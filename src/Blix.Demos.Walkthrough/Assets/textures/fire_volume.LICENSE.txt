fire_volume.bvol
================

Source:    JangaFX "Small Campfire" CC0 VDB animation pack, re-rendered as
           a packed dense 3D texture for Blix's volumetric fire path.

License:   CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
           https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original:  https://jangafx.com/software/embergen/download/free-vdb-animations
Author:    JangaFX (EmberGen simulation)

Pipeline:  Original was 200 OpenVDB files (single-frame, ~2MB each) covering
           a small campfire simulation. Conversion in tools/vdb_to_blix_volume.py:
             - reads the 'flames' (temperature/emission) grid
             - resamples each frame to a 48^3 lattice
             - takes every 6th frame -> 34 frames total
             - axis-swaps so EmberGen's Z-up maps to Blix's Y-up
             - normalises to global-max and quantises to R8
             - packs all 34 frames along the depth axis -> 48 x 48 x 1632
           Output is ~3.6MB R8 raw bytes with a 40-byte BVOL header.

           To regenerate or retune (different frame stride, resolution, or
           source pack), re-run the converter; see its docstring for
           parameters.
